About George Lee Engineering

Built on Precision.
Driven by Quality.

George Lee Engineering and Welding LTD is led by George Lee, an expert welder and fabricator based in North Wales with extensive hands-on experience in some of the most demanding environments in the industry — from food-grade production facilities to large-scale construction sites.

We combine specialist knowledge with a practical, client-first approach. Whether you need precise hygienic pipework systems, custom stainless steel fabrication, or certified structural welding on-site, we deliver results that meet the highest standards — on time, every time.

What We Do

Our Specialist Services

Process & Hygienic Pipework

Expert design, fabrication, and installation of stainless steel pipework systems to the highest hygienic standards — essential for food, beverage, dairy, and pharmaceutical environments.

  • Hygienic & dairy pipework
  • Food-grade standards compliance
  • Process pipework & systems
  • Full installation & testing

Stainless Steel Fabrication

Bespoke TIG-welded stainless steel components crafted to precise specifications — delivering clean, durable finishes for demanding applications across multiple sectors.

  • Tanks, vessels & hoppers
  • Platforms, walkways & guards
  • Custom bespoke components
  • Architectural & commercial

Structural Welding & Site Work

Certified structural welding using MIG and Stick processes for construction and civil engineering projects — fully equipped to mobilise for on-site work anywhere across North Wales and Chester.

  • MIG & Stick structural welding
  • Beams, columns & frameworks
  • On-site installation & repair
  • Modification & fabrication
